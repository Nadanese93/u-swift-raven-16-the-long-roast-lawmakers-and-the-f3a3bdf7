One satirical sentence for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7) ($2 prize, top 10 paid).

> The lawmaker who asked an AI to turn his bill into a PDF filed a bill to regulate it the same afternoon, on the grounds that it now knew too much about his files.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
